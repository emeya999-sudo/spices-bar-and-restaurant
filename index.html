The Story

Built around food.
Kept alive
by people.

In 2013, Chef Cristian Duhalde Diaz took over the space on Balarabe Musa Crescent and began shaping what La Taverna would become — a restaurant rooted in the flavours of Spain, Italy and Chile, built for people who think a meal should last as long as the company allows.

Over the years, La Taverna has grown into one of Victoria Island's most characterful dining destinations. Not through hype — through consistency, warmth and a genuine love of feeding people well.

The Culinary Trilogy

Three Countries. One Table. Lagos.

A distinct culinary trilogy curated by Chef Cristian Duhalde Diaz, rooted in Mediterranean and South American traditions and brought alive in Victoria Island.

01 / IBERIAN HERITAGE

ESPAÑA

Tapas · Paella · Sangria

Iberian sharing plates, crisp plantain empanadas, saffron rice in wide pans and the ease of long afternoon gatherings.

02 / ITALIAN CRAFT

ITALIA

Pizza · Pasta · Wine

Handcrafted fettuccine rolled daily, stone-baked pizzas with blistered crusts and cellar wines poured for the table.

03 / PACIFIC INFLUENCE

CHILE

Seafood · Coastal Flavours · Reds

Pacific coast inspiration, fresh whole fish seasoned with house relishes, slow-braised meats and robust South American varietals.
